My Story

One question has shaped my entire career.

I began performing professionally at six years old. That early education in audience, emotion, and storytelling grew into a career spanning film, entertainment, interactive media, emerging technology, product strategy, enterprise software, live experiences, and electronic music culture.

Why do people connect with one experience over another?

That question led me into interactive television, video streaming, games, virtual and mixed reality, global content systems, product-market fit, and human-centered product development. Although the industries changed, the work did not: understand the audience, clarify the value, and design an experience people choose to care about.

Over time, I recognized that artists face many of the same challenges as products. Exceptional work can still struggle when the positioning is unclear, the story is fragmented, or the supporting systems do not exist. Sentient Productions was built to close that gap.
