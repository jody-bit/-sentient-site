Independent musicians are increasingly expected to operate as the artist, brand, marketer, booking operation, publisher, content studio, strategist, and business behind their creative work.

Sentient exists to help build the professional infrastructure around the art.

What We Build

Everything built to work together.

Rather than offering isolated services, we build one connected system — identity, website, audience, revenue, and career plan — so nothing has to start from scratch.

Independence

Being independent doesn’t mean doing everything alone. Independence means maintaining meaningful agency over the career you’re building — the right strategy, people, technology, and infrastructure should give you more capacity to create, not bury you underneath the business of creating.

Who This Is For

Talent is only the beginning.

You don’t need to live in Detroit or Michigan, make electronic music, make original music, already have a large audience, or already have a professional team.

A strong fit
  • Takes their creative work seriously
  • Wants a coherent, professional, long-term career
  • Responds, communicates, and follows through
  • Can accept useful feedback without collapsing or disappearing
  • Is willing to test ideas and measure outcomes
  • Builds their career from self-reliance, not dependency
Not a fit
  • Expects someone else to create the career
  • Needs constant chasing or motivation
  • Rejects strategy before trying it
  • Wants instant visibility without infrastructure
  • Is unwilling to document the process honestly
  • Expects others to carry the financial responsibilities of their career
