The Pilot

Great music deserves more than hope.

Most artists are expected to figure out branding, websites, bookings, releases, partnerships, and business strategy on their own. This pilot exists to test a better way.

The goal isn’t simply to help three artists. It’s to create something that can help hundreds more.

Probably not a fit
  • You’re looking for overnight success.
  • You only want free marketing.
  • You’re unwilling to invest time between sessions.
  • You’re not comfortable experimenting.
  • You’re primarily looking for management or booking representation.

Why This Exists

Detroit created one of the world’s most influential musical movements.

Too many Detroit artists still struggle to build careers beyond the city that created the culture, even as electronic music has become a global industry.

Detroit deserves to be represented by artists with careers as enduring as the music itself. This pilot is my contribution toward that future.

What We’re Building

An operating system for artist development.

Rather than offering isolated services, we’re testing an integrated operating system for artist development.
